# Code of conduct

This code applies to everyone who takes part in this repository: in issues,
pull requests, reviews, security reports and any other conversation here.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good intent, including when you disagree.
- Keep feedback about the work, not the person.
- Welcome people of every background, identity and level of experience.
- Keep private information private: yours, other people's, and anything from a
  Pulse workspace that is not meant to be public.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes or remarks.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly derailing a discussion, or continuing after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

Email [support@trypulse.tech](mailto:support@trypulse.tech) with "Code of
conduct" in the subject line. Tell us what happened, where, and who was
involved; links to the issue or comment help. Reports are read by the Pulse
team and kept confidential.

## What happens next

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, lock a conversation, or block someone
from the repository, depending on what happened. We will tell the person who
reported it what we did, where that is appropriate.

This code is based on the ideas of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
